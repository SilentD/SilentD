VulaCare

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Offline-first clinic management and queue orchestration.

VulaCare is a clinic management platform designed to improve patient flow and reduce unnecessary waiting through digital queue orchestration, multi-role workflows and offline-first functionality.

The system combines a web application, backend services, local data storage and real-time synchronization to support clinic operations in environments where connectivity cannot always be relied upon.
